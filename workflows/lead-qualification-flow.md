# Lead Qualification Flow

## 1. Capture

Collect lead details from a form, spreadsheet, or manual entry.

## 2. Validate

Check that required fields are present:

- Name or business name
- Contact method
- Service interest
- Timeline
- Notes

## 3. Qualify

Score the lead based on:

- Fit for the service
- Urgency
- Budget signal
- Clear next action

## 4. Route

Route the lead into:

- High-priority follow-up
- Nurture list
- Needs more information
- Not a fit
